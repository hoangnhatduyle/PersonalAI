## Role Overview

Hoang joined the CincySI Lab in October 2026, shortly after starting his M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Cincinnati. The lab, led by Professor Justin Zhan, works across AI and data science. Hoang's research interests there are AI, multi-agent systems, and large language models (LLMs).

His research is about making multi-agent LLM systems accountable: when several LLM agents exchange messages and reach a decision, can we check what they claim against what actually happened?

---

## Current research (high level)

Two manuscripts written at CincySI Lab have been submitted to **NAACL 2027** and are **currently under review**.

- **Manuscript A — evidence in multi-agent decisions:** auditing whether the evidence that multi-agent LLM systems cite is what actually drove their decisions.
- **Manuscript B — confidence across agents:** how an agent's expressed confidence changes as claims are passed from one agent to the next in a multi-agent LLM pipeline.

Neither paper has been accepted or published yet.

---

## Connection to production experience

This research grows out of Hoang's industrial background (MES and shop-floor systems at Nysus Solutions and First Solar), where decisions made by automated systems had to be traceable and trustworthy. It also connects to his hands-on agent projects, including AEGIS (an agent firewall that won the Best Use of Auth0 prize at MakeUC 2026) and the multi-agent trading platform prototype.
